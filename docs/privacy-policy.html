This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Serpent ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android game.

1. Information We Collect

Neon Serpent does not directly collect personal information. However, the app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect the following data:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device type, operating system version, and language
  • Interaction data with advertisements

2. How We Use Information

We do not use your personal data directly. AdMob uses the data it collects to serve relevant advertisements. Game progress (scores, settings, unlocks) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob – used to display ads. Google's privacy policy applies to data collected by AdMob. You can review it at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Advertising ID and Opt-Out

AdMob uses the Android Advertising ID to serve personalised ads. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.

5. Data Storage

All game data (high scores, settings, purchased items) is stored locally on your device using Android's standard storage. We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

6. Children's Privacy

Neon Serpent is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
